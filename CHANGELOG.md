## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added
- First release as **Aurora Creature**: exploration, catching and turn-based battles with original creatures, built with TypeScript and Canvas 2D.
- 3 regions with 3 starters each, 7 main areas, trainers, the rival Kauã and 3 gyms with badges.
- Experience, new moves and level-based evolution; team of up to 6 creatures.
- Shelter, Market, START menu, trainer card and autosave with 3 slots.
- English and Portuguese, synthesized soundtrack and sound effects, and touch controls for phones and tablets.
